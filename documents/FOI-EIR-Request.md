# Hexham to Corbridge Active Travel Corridor — information request

Status: checked draft, not sent. Reviewed 6 October 2026.

**Subject: FOI / EIR request – Hexham to Corbridge Active Travel Corridor: costs, programme and risks**

Dear Information Governance Team,

Please provide the recorded information below about the Hexham to Corbridge Active Travel Corridor, also described as the Tynedale walking, wheeling and cycling route.

Please handle environmental information under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, and any remaining information under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

This request concerns the Hexham–Corbridge scheme specifically. Where funding or records cover both this scheme and the Bedlington corridor, please identify the Hexham–Corbridge allocation separately where held.

For current figures and forecasts, please provide the latest recorded information held when you receive this request, stating its reporting date. Existing reports, schedules or documents are welcome; I am not asking the council to commission new assessments.

## 1. Budget and funding

(a) The original approved scheme budget and the current approved budget, with a breakdown by funding source, including Levelling Up Fund grant, Northumberland County Council funding and other sources.

(b) The amount of council funding allocated specifically to Hexham–Corbridge, distinguishing the original contribution from any subsequent additional allocation.

(c) The reports or decision records approving the original budget and subsequent changes.

(d) Please state whether the figures include design and project management, land acquisition and compensation, legal costs, utilities, bridge works and contingency, and whether VAT is included.

## 2. Spend and forecast

(a) Total recorded expenditure to the latest reporting date, broken down by phase where held, together with any separately recorded scheme-wide costs.

(b) The latest forecast final cost for each phase and the scheme as a whole, identifying any costs excluded from that forecast.

(c) The original contingency/risk allowance, the amount drawn down or allocated to date, and the remaining allowance, where recorded.

## 3. Budget changes and contractor claims

(a) Any recorded actual or forecast overspend against the original approved budget and against the current approved budget.

(b) Reports, briefings or decision records proposing or approving additional funding, including from the council’s capital programme, reserves or borrowing, and the recorded funding source.

(c) The latest recorded aggregate values of contractor variations, compensation events and delay/prolongation claims, distinguishing amounts submitted, agreed and paid where held. Please clarify whether agreed amounts are included in the expenditure and final-cost forecasts supplied under section 2.

## 4. Programme and Phase 1 status

(a) The original approved programme and the latest programme or schedule for each phase, showing planned and actual or forecast construction start and completion dates.

(b) The recorded boundaries of each phase, including any changes to those boundaries.

(c) For Phase 1, the dates on which it opened to users and was formally certified or recorded as complete, where applicable, together with the relevant completion certificate or equivalent record.

(d) The latest recorded list of outstanding Phase 1 works or defects and their forecast completion dates, including any remaining items after opening or completion certification.

(e) The main recorded reasons for changes to the programme.

## 5. Park Well layby traffic restrictions

For the layby at Park Well on the A695/Corbridge Road, please provide:

(a) Copies of the temporary traffic regulation orders and associated location plans relating to its closure or use as a scheme site compound.

(b) Any replacement orders, extensions or authorisations extending the restriction, including any extension from 12 to 24 months if such an extension exists.

(c) The recorded reasons for the original restriction and any extension, together with the latest anticipated reopening date.

Please distinguish the legal duration of each order from the anticipated period of actual closure. If the council uses a different formal name or road reference for this layby, please identify it.

## 6. Dilston / Devil’s Water bridge

This section concerns the A695 bridge over the Devil’s Water at Dilston, listed by Historic England as “Bridge Over the Devils Water”, list entry 1370545.

(a) The latest approved drawings for the bridge alterations associated with the scheme.

(b) Any heritage impact assessment or heritage statement, and any recorded assessment of whether listed building consent or other heritage-related consent is required.

(c) Copies of any relevant applications and decisions, and correspondence with Historic England or the council’s conservation officers concerning the proposed alterations.

(d) Any existing assessment of the cost or programme implications of delayed approval, redesign or challenge to the bridge proposals.

## 7. Risks and grant conditions

(a) The latest project risk register and any separate workstream risk registers covering Hexham–Corbridge, including recorded cost, design, land acquisition, bridge, programme and funding risks.

(b) The grant agreement or award conditions, including subsequent variations, relevant to eligible expenditure, spending and completion deadlines, cost overruns, incomplete delivery and repayment or clawback.

(c) Any written approval of revised spending or completion deadlines, and any written assurance or decision from the funding body concerning continued funding despite programme delays. Please provide the latest relevant correspondence if a request for an extension remains unresolved.

Please respond against the numbered items above. Where information is not held, please identify the relevant item. Where information is already published, precise document links and relevant page or section references are welcome.

If any information is withheld, please identify the applicable exemption or exception, explain its application, and disclose the remaining information where possible.

If you consider that the request would exceed the applicable FOIA cost limit, or would be manifestly unreasonable under the EIR because of the burden involved, please identify which parts cause the difficulty and provide advice and assistance on how to narrow it. My priority is the budget, expenditure, final-cost forecast, programme and approved grant deadlines.

I am happy to receive the information electronically. Please acknowledge receipt and respond within the applicable statutory timescale, normally 20 working days. If an extension is applied, please explain the basis and confirm the revised response date.
