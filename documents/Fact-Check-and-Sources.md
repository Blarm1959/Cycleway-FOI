## FOI / EIR handling

Much of the request is likely to be environmental information, including financial records relating to highway works. This is an assessment, not a binding determination; the council must apply the appropriate regime.

EIR has no fixed FOIA-style cost ceiling, although a manifestly unreasonable burden may justify refusal. The usual response period is 20 working days; an EIR extension to 40 working days is available where volume and complexity justify it. The request asks for existing recorded information and allows documents to answer the questions.

Sources:

- [ICO: What are the EIR?](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/eir-and-access-to-information/guide-to-the-environmental-information-regulations/what-are-the-eir/)
- [ICO: Manifestly unreasonable requests](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/foi/freedom-of-information-and-environmental-information-regulations/regulation-12-4-b-manifestly-unreasonable-requests/)
- [ICO: EIR compliance time limits](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/foi/freedom-of-information-and-environmental-information-regulations/time-limits-for-compliance-under-the-eir/)
